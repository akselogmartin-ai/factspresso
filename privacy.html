Factspresso Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Factspresso publishes short fact videos on its own social media accounts (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook). To publish them, we use our own small publishing tool, "IRIS". This policy explains what that tool does with data.

Who we are

Factspresso is an independent creator channel based in Denmark. Contact: akselogmartin@gmail.com

What the tool accesses

The tool connects only to Factspresso's own accounts, after we, the account owners, have signed in and allowed it. It uses that access only to upload and publish our own videos to our own accounts, and to read basic statistics about our own videos (views, likes, comments) so we can see which topics work.

What we store

The access tokens for our own accounts, stored on our own computer (not in any shared or cloud folder) and never shared with anyone. The IDs and statistics of our own published videos, in internal notes on our own computer.

What we do not do

We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data about viewers, followers or other users. We do not use tracking, cookies or advertising tools in the publishing tool. We do not access anyone else's accounts or content.

Data from the platforms

Use of YouTube is subject to the Google Privacy Policy. Our use of YouTube API Services follows the YouTube Terms of Service. TikTok, Instagram and Facebook have their own privacy policies.

Removing access

We can revoke the tool's access to our accounts at any time in each platform's security settings, after which the stored tokens stop working. You can contact us at any time with questions about this policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above.
